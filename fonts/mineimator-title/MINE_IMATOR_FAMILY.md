# Mine-imator Family

Standalone Minecraft Title Generator font family for Mine-imator/Modelbench-specific glyph shapes.

- Lowercase `h` and `o` keep the standard glyphs.
- Uppercase `H` maps to the custom variant (`capital_h.json`, U+E000 internally).
- Uppercase `O` maps to the custom variant (`capital_o.json`, U+E001 internally).
- The only texture preset currently included is `mineimator.png`.
- Additional Mine-imator-only glyphs can be added as we find them.
